Finding the Perfect Grind

The journey to a perfect shot begins with the particle size. For espresso, we seek a consistency similar to fine table salt. If your grind is too coarse, water will rush through, resulting in a sour, under-extracted cup.

error

Too Coarse

Under-extracted. Tastes sour, salty, and lacks body.

warning

Too Fine

Over-extracted. Tastes bitter, ashy, and hollow.

The Art of Tamping

Consistency is key. Apply 30 lbs of even pressure to create a flat, level bed. This prevents "channeling"—where water finds the path of least resistance.

  • check_circle Ensure the portafilter is dry
  • check_circle Level the grounds before tamping
